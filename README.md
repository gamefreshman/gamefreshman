## Research interests

My main interest is in **action-conditioned prediction of future task and interaction states**: how an agent can anticipate the consequences of its actions and use those predictions to make better decisions.

- **Proactive and long-horizon agents:** intervention timing, intent and task-state tracking, and decisions under incomplete information.
- **Agent systems and harnesses:** tool use, memory, workflow orchestration, and recovery when assumptions or intermediate results change.
- **Evaluation:** separating state estimation, consequence prediction, and action selection to understand where a system succeeds or fails.

I also have experience with retrieval-augmented systems and diffusion models for molecular generation.

## Selected research & projects

### Proactive Intent World Model (PIWM)
*First author / lead developer · March 2026–present*

I developed a framework for modeling customer states, candidate assistance actions, and subsequent responses in retail interactions. The project includes **GuidanceSalesBench**, structured-state modeling, and evaluation of both intervention and non-intervention decisions.

A central question is whether predicting an action's consequences actually improves action selection. Our latest manuscript examines this gap, including the difficulty of deciding when to remain silent.

**[See Better, Foresee Better, Act Wiser: Physically Grounded Proactive Modeling and Decision Making](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03371)**  
Honghui Zhang et al., 2026 · Preprint  
[Code and evaluation tooling](https://github.com/gamefreshman/ProactiveIntentWorldModel)

### AI Tendering and Bidding System — HKUST
*Research assistant · October 2025–February 2026*

I led the initial system architecture and built a **LangGraph multi-agent workflow** for processing long tender documents, retrieving relevant information, and tracing evidence to its source. My work included document chunking and agent behavior tracking, supporting an initial enterprise pilot.

### DisCo: Joint Discrete and Continuous Molecular Diffusion
*Lead developer · September 2025–present*

I implemented a joint diffusion model for discrete molecular features—atom types and bonds—and continuous 3D coordinates. I also explored **direct preference optimization** for target molecular properties. The project has a working training pipeline; stable gains from preference optimization remain an open experimental question.

### GraphRAG Regulatory Compliance Review
*Project lead · November 2024–November 2025*

I built an updatable knowledge graph from regulations, cases, and industry standards, combining keyword search with semantic retrieval. The system uses OCR and document structuring to support evidence-based review of occupational health reports.
